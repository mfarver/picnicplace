# Camera frames for vision tests

Real frames, kept to check the vision functions on what cameras actually see.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `vls6045ex-bottom.webp` | A TDK VLS6045EX wound inductor (6 × 6 mm) on a nozzle, from a LumenPnP's bottom camera, 1920 × 1080, 0.01305 mm per pixel (the machine's calibration). Its two terminals are 1.7 × 4.8 mm, 2.6 mm apart; the copper wire ends shine below them. |
| `b5b-ph-sm4-tb-bottom.webp` | A JST B5B-PH-SM4-TB connector (2.0 mm pitch, five leads and two anchor tabs, top entry, SMT) on a nozzle, from the same machine, 1848 × 1080. Its ivory nylon body is nearly as bright as its tin leads, and the leads stand out 2 mm past one side of the body, so the pads' center is not the body's. The five leads span 651 px for their 8.0 mm, 0.01229 mm per pixel: finer than the camera's calibration, so the frame was likely resized when it was saved. The part is turned about 1.6°. |
