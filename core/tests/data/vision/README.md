# Camera frames for vision tests

Real frames, kept to check the vision functions on what cameras actually see.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `vls6045ex-bottom.webp` | A TDK VLS6045EX wound inductor (6 × 6 mm) on a nozzle, from a LumenPnP's bottom camera, 1920 × 1080, 0.01305 mm per pixel (the machine's calibration). Its two terminals are 1.7 × 4.8 mm, 2.6 mm apart; the copper wire ends shine below them. |
| `b5b-ph-sm4-tb-bottom.webp` | A JST B5B-PH-SM4-TB connector (2.0 mm pitch, five leads and two anchor tabs, top entry, SMT) on a nozzle, from the same machine, 1848 × 1080. Its ivory nylon body is nearly as bright as its tin leads, and the leads stand out 2 mm past one side of the body, so the pads' center is not the body's. The five leads span 651 px for their 8.0 mm, 0.01229 mm per pixel: finer than the camera's calibration, so the frame was likely resized when it was saved. The part is turned about 1.6°. |
| `lumenpnp-n24-z-4.63.png`, `lumenpnp-n24-z-6.49.png` | An empty N24 nozzle tip (2.4 mm hole, 4.0 mm end) from a LumenPnP's bottom camera, 0.06502 mm per pixel (the machine's calibration), the middle 400 × 400 pixels of 1920 × 1080 frames, with the nozzle at Z -4.63 and -6.49: the camera's depth of field is wide enough that they barely differ. Round the tip's dark hole and lit end is a lit ring about 8.4 mm across, the size of an N75's end. |
| `lumenpnp-n24-left.png`, `lumenpnp-n08-right.png` | The same machine's bottom camera, 0.06218 mm per pixel, the middle 400 × 400 pixels, from a flight recording of naming the tips: an empty N24 on the left nozzle, and an empty N08 (0.8 mm hole, 1.5 mm end) on the right. Round the N08's dimly lit end is a brighter ring about 4.2 mm across, with the N08's end inside it about half as bright: it looks like an N24, except that its "hole" is not dark. |
| `lumenpnp-datum-top.webp` | A LumenPnP's datum board from its top camera: 20 frames (0.0105 mm per pixel) over a grid, stitched at 0.05 mm per pixel, from X 168 to 268 and Y 166 to 214 (picture right is +X, down is -Y). Its dot is at (218.107, 197.304); the board faces the bottom camera, toward -Y. A tinned pad and a silkscreen disc, each 5 mm across, lie 22.5 mm either side of the dot and 7.5 mm toward the camera: the pad is whiter than the paper disc lying below the dot, with a torn tab. |
| `n045-focus/z*.webp` | An empty N045 on a LumenPnP's bottom camera, the middle 480 × 480 pixels (about 0.038 mm per pixel; its hole is about 18 pixels right of and 15 below the middle), with the nozzle at raw Z -24.30 to -27.30 in 0.25 mm steps (the file name). Scored round the tip's end the sharpness peaks near -26.5; scored over 8 mm, which takes in the nozzle's larger rings higher up, it climbs all the way down. |
| `lumenpnp-n24-hole-x-1.webp`, `lumenpnp-n24-hole-x+1.webp`, `lumenpnp-n24-hole-turned.webp` | Whole 1920 × 1080 frames from the same LumenPnP's bottom camera, from a flight recording of calibrating it with an empty N24: near the focus with the head stepped 1 mm each way in X (raw Z -25.6), and at the focus turned 180° (raw Z -26.52, about 0.0362 mm per pixel). Behind the tip's dark hole and gray end is a lit flange about 10 mm across, farther from the camera, which moves about two thirds as far in the picture as the tip's end when the head moves. |
| `lumenpnp-n24-focus-left.webp`, `lumenpnp-n75-focus-right.webp` | Whole frames from the same bottom camera at its focus as calibrated by the N24's hole (0.03469 mm per pixel): an empty N24 on the left nozzle at raw Z -27.46, and an empty N75 on the right at the end of its travel (raw Z -28.49). The N24's 2.4 mm hole is dark, and its end looks 4.9 mm across. The N75's end is 9.2 to 9.4 mm; its 7.5 mm cup is lit inside, with a darker circle about 5 mm across and the nozzle's own 1.5 mm bore at its bottom. |
